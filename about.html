About OM STORE

OM STORE is a demo marketplace concept for browsing Free Fire account listings. This sample site uses local browser storage and does not process payments or verify accounts.

For a real marketplace, account ownership, payment, dispute handling, and account-transfer policies need to be implemented and reviewed on a secure server. Check the game publisher's current terms before buying or selling accounts.
